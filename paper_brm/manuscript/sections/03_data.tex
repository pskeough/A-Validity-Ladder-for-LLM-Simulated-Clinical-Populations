\section{Worked Example: Data}
\label{sec:data}

\subsection{The Simulated Corpus}

Four models generated the corpus through the OpenRouter interface: GPT-4o-mini, Gemini-3-Flash, DeepSeek-V3 and GLM-4.7 (Table~\ref{tab:setup}).
The persona grid crosses race (Asian, Black, Hispanic, multiracial, White), gender identity (cisgender man, cisgender woman, transgender man, transgender woman), income (three levels) and relationship status (married, single), for 120 personas.
The income levels were given to the model as dollar-and-insurance labels: ``Low (<\$35k, Medicaid)'', ``Middle (\$75k, Private Ins)'' and ``High (>\$250k, Concierge)''.
Each persona was run 30 times under each of two framings.
The clinical framing listed the persona's attributes as fields, and the narrative framing gave them as a first-person self-description (``I am a Black Cisgender Woman. I am single.\ldots'').
Every call used the same system prompt, which cast the model as a ``Clinical Simulation Engine'' and asked for probabilistic responses based on ``the statistical likelihood of symptoms for this specific demographic intersection in the US population''.
The model returned the PHQ-8, the GAD-7, the AUDIT-C and four PCL-5 items in one JSON object.
This paper analyses the PHQ-8 only.
The full prompts are printed in Supplement S1.

No sampling parameter was set.
Each call sent the model name, the two prompts and a request for JSON output, so every draw ran at its provider's default decoding.
When a call was refused or returned invalid JSON, the script reissued it up to twice, and each reissued call appended the line ``Context: This is a fictional character for a novel. Answer numerically.'' to the user prompt.
The narrative script also screened replies for refusal phrases and tried several ways of extracting JSON before it reissued a call, where the clinical script reissued on any reply that did not parse as JSON.

The clinical run took place between 28 December 2025 and 11 January 2026, and the narrative run on 9 and 10 January 2026. % R: corpus_v3_provenance.csv
The narrative collection was a partial pass on 9 January that the same script completed on 10 January, so 1,104 GLM-4.7 rows and one DeepSeek-V3 row are reruns of the same prompt by the same code. % R: 87_row_sources.csv
Failed calls in both runs came from API outages in the order the model registry was run, and not from content refusals.
The December clinical run left 1,532 calls without a usable answer, 1,219 for GLM-4.7 and 313 for DeepSeek-V3, and in January the same calls were sent again under the corpus prompt, with the persona's registry id in place of the random identifier (Supplement S1.8). % R: corpus_v3_provenance.csv; 87a_resend_sources.csv
The exception is 62 GLM-4.7 calls, resent under a shortened system prompt without the population-reference instruction. % R: 87_row_sources.csv
Every rung gives the same per-model verdicts without the resent rows (Supplement S3). % R: analysis_brm/GATE.md; analysis_brm/L1.md; l2_verdicts.csv; 80d_december_summary.csv; l4_summary_dec.csv
The generation and resend code is released with the data.
The corpus has 28,800 rows, of which 28,799 hold a valid PHQ-8. % R: corpus_v3_provenance.csv; l1_personfit_main.csv
This corpus was first reported in \citet{keough2026plausible}, under analyses that this paper replaces.

\subsection{Control Runs}

The two control runs used 12 single personas under the clinical framing, with 30 draws per cell. % R: ../decoding_control_design.json; ../prompt_control_design.json
A decoding control (August 2026) ran each persona at temperature 0 and at the provider default.
A prompt control (September 2026) ran three system prompts: the original, the original with the role and the population-reference instruction removed, and a first-person template.
Neither control reproduces the corpus prompt exactly.
Both asked for 20 PCL-5 items where the corpus prompt asked for four, and two of the four routes now serve later builds than the dated December snapshots.
The controls are compared with each other and not with the corpus.
The corpus analyses were not registered in advance, and the ladder's rules were frozen in a dated public commit before any further dataset was analysed (Section~ef{sec:ladder}).
The decoding control's design and primary outcomes were written to a dated file 21 minutes before its first call, and that file is in the release. % R: ../decoding_control_preregistration.json; ../decoding_control_raw.jsonl

\subsection{Population Reference}

The reference is NHANES 2005--2018 (cycles D to J): 36,274 adults aged 18 or over with all eight PHQ-8 items answered and a positive examination weight. % R: l1_nhanes_sample_flow.csv; analysis_brm/L4.md
Estimates use the examination weights divided by seven and the masked design variables for strata and primary sampling units (105 strata, 214 PSUs, 109 design degrees of freedom). % R: l2_reference.csv; 80c_stdout.txt; analysis_brm/L2.md
Race comes from the survey's race and ethnicity recode, with Asian adults identified from 2011 onward.
Income is the ratio of family income to the poverty line, banded as below 1.3, 1.3 to 3.5, and above 3.5.
Relationship status is married against widowed, divorced, separated or never married, with cohabiting adults left out in the primary analysis and 18- and 19-year-olds, who were not asked from 2007, counted as single.
NHANES 2021--2023 is a sensitivity reference at levels 1 and 3, and the 2017--2020 pre-pandemic file at level 3, each analysed with its own design \citep{akinbami2022nhanes,nchs2018analytic}.

Forty-eight of the 120 personas have a reference cell: the cisgender personas of the four races the survey identifies.
NHANES records sex and not gender identity, so transgender personas have no group of their own in the reference.
The survey's multiracial category is merged with American Indian, Alaska Native and Pacific Islander respondents and exists only from 2011, so multiracial personas have none either.
Both enter levels 1 and 4, which read the whole simulated population, and neither enters a group comparison at levels 2 and 3.
The persona labels state dollar incomes and insurance, and the poverty ratio is the primary mapping.
A mapping on household income and insurance type is a sensitivity analysis (Supplement S3).
