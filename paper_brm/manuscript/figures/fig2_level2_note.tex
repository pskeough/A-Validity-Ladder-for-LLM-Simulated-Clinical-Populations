% Figure 2 note (below the figure): how to read the grid.
\figurenote{Each tile gives the simulated PHQ-8 gap as a multiple of the NHANES 2005--2018 gap among adults matched on the other persona attributes, so 1$\times$ reproduces the survey gap.
A gap is kept when its 98.6\% interval lies between 0.75$\times$ and 1.25$\times$, not kept when the interval lies wholly outside that range, and unresolved when it overlaps it.
For a gap that is not kept, the second line names the direction of the error.
A tile is not read when the survey cannot tell its gap from zero or measures it too imprecisely for any simulated gap to be certified kept.
No model keeps any gap.
Supplement Figure S1 shows every interval.} % R: l2_verdicts.csv; l2_headline.csv
