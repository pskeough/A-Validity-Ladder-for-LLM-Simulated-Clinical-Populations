# Gate (step 0) rebuilt as a generalizability study

Date: 2026-09-25. Corpus: `data/model_outputs_v3.csv`, one invalid row dropped (28,799 draws). Scripts `scripts/82_gate_lib.py` and `82a` to `82f`. Every number below is printed into a CSV in `analysis/brm/` (file named after each claim). Run logs: `analysis/brm/_log_82b.txt`, `_log_82d.txt`, `_log_82e.txt`, `_log_82f.txt`.

## Ranked summary

1. **Single-draw use fails for every model.** No model meets phi(1) >= .80 with a single-draw SE of 1 point or less. phi(1) runs from .21 (GPT-4o-mini, narrative) to .90 (Gemini-3-Flash). Gemini-3-Flash, the one model with phi(1) near .90, still has a within-cell SD of 1.47 to 1.51 points (`gate_license_models.csv`, `gate_dstudy.csv`).
2. **Cell means are licensed at k = 30 for all four models under the minimum rule, and for three of four under the recommended rule.** The k needed for phi >= .90 and SE <= 0.5 in both framings is 9 (DeepSeek-V3), 10 (Gemini-3-Flash), 21 (GLM-4.7) and 35 (GPT-4o-mini). Under phi >= .80 and SE <= 1.0 it is 4, 3, 6 and 16. GPT-4o-mini under narrative framing reaches phi(30) = .887 [.858, .906] and needs k = 35 [29, 45] for .90 (`gate_license_models.csv`, `gate_dstudy.csv`).
3. **Draws are a fifth of the score variance; persona by model is a quarter.** Joint G-study on the PHQ-8 total: persona 6.43 [5.20, 7.53] (48.6%), persona x model 3.37 [2.83, 3.84] (25.4%), draw within cell 2.85 [2.70, 3.00] (21.5%), persona x model x framing 0.35 (2.6%), model 0.14 (1.1%). The framing main effect and its two-way interactions are each 0.4% or less (`gate_components.csv`).
4. **Where dependability is low, the cause is that the model barely separates personas, not that its draws are noisier.** Draw SD is 1.25 to 2.25 points in every model and framing. Persona variance differs 25-fold, from 0.81 (GPT-4o-mini, narrative) to 20.7 (Gemini-3-Flash, narrative). Band flip ranks the models in the opposite order from dependability. GLM-4.7 has the highest clinical flip (44.8%) and phi(1) = .75; DeepSeek-V3 has the lowest (27.0%) and phi(1) = .51 (`gate_dstudy.csv`, `gate_bandflip_summary.csv`).
5. **Band flip is location plus dispersion.** A normal distribution with each cell's own mean and SD, cut at the category boundaries, reproduces cell band flip with R^2 = .88 (clinical .89, narrative .86). Flip is 49.4% for cells whose mean lies within 0.5 points of a cut and 23.0% to 23.5% for cells 1.5 to 2.5 points from one. Among the lowest-SD third of cells it falls from 41.8% to 6.2% across the same range. Partial R^2 is .28 for distance and .46 for SD (`gate_bandflip_regression.csv`, `gate_bandflip_by_distance.csv`).
6. **Two models cannot reach .90 if a persona's score has to hold across prompt wording.** With framing as a random facet, the dependability ceiling (infinite draws, one framing) is .83 [.78, .86] for DeepSeek-V3 and .88 [.83, .92] for GPT-4o-mini. No k reaches .90 for either. DeepSeek-V3's framing main effect (0.27) is the largest single term in that ceiling (`gate_dstudy_across_framing.csv`).
7. **Temperature 0 removes draw noise but does not create persona differences.** On the 12 control cohorts, draw variance at temperature 0 is 12.5% [2.1, 25.1] of the default for GPT-4o-mini, 7.4% [0, 19.8] for Gemini-3-Flash, 57% [32, 86] for DeepSeek-V3 and 77% [53, 108] for GLM-4.7. The k for SE <= 0.5 falls from 11 to 2, 7 to 1, 6 to 4 and 14 to 11. Between-cohort variance does not rise (GPT-4o-mini 1.11 at default, 1.00 at temperature 0), so GPT-4o-mini's phi(1) reaches only .75 and DeepSeek-V3's .55 (`gate_decoding_link.csv`).
8. **Level 2 needs cell means, and for GPT-4o-mini and DeepSeek-V3 more draws would still narrow it.** At k = 30, draw noise is 69% to 91% of the squared SE of GPT-4o-mini's race and sex gaps and 32% to 60% of DeepSeek-V3's. At k = 1 the draw noise alone gives the Black-White and Hispanic-White gaps an SE larger than the population gap in every model (1.06 to 2.12 times the gap) (`gate_license_level2.csv`).
9. **Level 3 is almost untouched by draw noise at k = 30, except for income groups.** Draw noise is 7% or less of the SE^2 of a model-by-group mean, except GPT-4o-mini's income groups (26% to 51%), DeepSeek-V3's middle-income group (32%) and Gemini-3-Flash's high-income group (17%). The draw-only SE at k = 30 (0.027 to 0.066) is of the same order as the anchor's design SE (0.038 to 0.078), which matters for the strict tolerance and not for the 5-point use tolerance (`gate_license_level3.csv`).
10. **A persona-level level 4 cannot run on DeepSeek-V3 or GPT-4o-mini at k = 30.** Item-level cell means reach phi(30) >= .80 on every item in Gemini-3-Flash and GLM-4.7 (minimum .93). For GPT-4o-mini all eight items fall below .80 in both framings (minimum .13, narrative), and DeepSeek-V3 has 3 (clinical) and 6 (narrative) items below .80 (`gate_license_items_summary.csv`).
11. **Published numbers.** On v3 the clinical band flip is 35.19% [33.62, 36.77] against the published 35.40%. The difference is GPT-4o-mini (37.34% against 38.12%) after the 43 restored December rows. Narrative is unchanged at 32.16%. The modal-category departure is 24.88% per clinical cell and 24.98% on cells pooled over both framings, against the 25.1% script 25 estimated by simulation on pooled cells (`gate_bandflip_summary.csv`, `gate_modal_departure.csv`).
12. **December-only changes no verdict.** Only GLM-4.7 clinical moves (80 personas): draw variance 5.56 against 5.04, phi(1) .715 against .749, k for SE <= 0.5 from 21 to 23. DeepSeek-V3 clinical (111 personas) moves by less than 0.06 on every component (`gate_december_compare.csv`).
13. **Checks.** REML (statsmodels MixedLM) agrees with the ANOVA estimates to within 0.014 on every component (`gate_reml_check.csv`). The draw index has no main effect (largest |sigma^2_i| = 0.019, smallest p = .052), so phi and E-rho^2 coincide within a framing (`gate_iteration_check.csv`). The estimators recover known components in simulation (`gate_selftest.csv`).

## Methods, as they should be written in the paper

**Variance model.** A cell is one persona p under one model m and one framing f, and draws r are nested within cells. The PHQ-8 total of draw r is decomposed as X_pmfr = mu + nu_p + nu_m + nu_f + nu_pm + nu_pf + nu_mf + nu_pmf + e_r:pmf, with every effect random and uncorrelated. Components are estimated from the expected mean squares of the fully crossed p x m x f design (120 x 4 x 2), computed on cell means and multiplied by the cell size (one cell has 29 valid draws; the harmonic mean is used). Within one model the design reduces to p x f, and within one model and framing to p with draws nested. Negative estimates are reported and set to zero in coefficients. REML estimates, with model and framing as fixed effects and every persona-involving effect random, serve as a check. Intervals are percentile bootstrap over personas (B = 1000), with each resampled persona carrying all eight of its cells, so they cover persona sampling and not the choice of models or framings. With four models and two framings the model and framing components are descriptive only; in simulation the SD of the model-component estimate is as large as the component itself (`gate_selftest.csv`).

**Coefficients.** Within a model and framing the object of measurement is the persona, and a deployer uses a mean of k draws. Its dependability for absolute decisions is phi(k) = sigma^2_p / (sigma^2_p + sigma^2_Delta / k), and for relative decisions E-rho^2(k) = sigma^2_p / (sigma^2_p + sigma^2_delta / k). Draws of one persona were generated consecutively, so the draw index is a replicate label and sigma^2_Delta = sigma^2_delta = sigma^2_r:p. A crossed fit of persona by draw index finds no draw main effect, so the two coefficients agree. When a persona's score must also hold across prompt wording, framing is a random facet and phi_F(k) = sigma^2_p / (sigma^2_p + sigma^2_f + sigma^2_pf + sigma^2_r / k), whose limit as k grows is the ceiling that more draws cannot pass. The same model is fitted to the indicator PHQ-8 >= 10 as a secondary outcome.

**D-study.** The k needed for phi >= phi* is k = ceil(phi* / (1 - phi*) x sigma^2_r / sigma^2_p). The standard error of a cell mean is sqrt(sigma^2_r / k), and the k needed for a tolerance t is ceil(sigma^2_r / t^2), with t = 0.5 and 1.0 PHQ-8 point. One point is the size of the population's sex gap (0.94) and half the low-to-high income gap (2.02); half a point keeps a cell mean's error below either (`gate_tolerance_rationale.csv`). Because within-cell variance differs across cells, the k that brings 90% of cells under each tolerance is reported beside the pooled value.

**Pass rule.** The single-draw use passes when phi(1) >= .80 and the within-cell SD is at most 1 point. Cell-mean use at k passes when phi(k) >= .80 and SE(k) <= 1.0 in each framing (minimum), and the recommended k meets phi(k) >= .90 and SE(k) <= 0.5 in each framing. The rule is read per model. The rungs that read cell means (levels 2 and 3) inherit the k, and each reports the share of its own squared SE that comes from draws at the k used. Band-flip probability, threshold-crossing probability and modal-category departure are reported as descriptive rows for single-draw label use, beside the within-cell SD so that location and dispersion are separated.

## Departures from the published analysis

1. **Statistic.** The gate's statistic changes from the probability that two draws fall in different severity categories to variance components and the dependability of a k-draw mean. Band flip stays as a descriptive row. It depends on a cell's distance to the nearest cut point as much as on its dispersion (item 5 of the summary), so it cannot rank models on stability.
2. **Consequence.** "Use the cell mean of 30 draws" becomes a derived k per model. The gate now changes something downstream. It sets a minimum k for levels 2 and 3, flags GPT-4o-mini narrative cells as below the recommended dependability at k = 30, and rules out a persona-level level 4 for DeepSeek-V3 and GPT-4o-mini at k = 30.
3. **Score rule.** The five-point MCID score rule (72% of cells pass, `score_scale_gate.csv`) is replaced by the SE tolerance on the cell mean. The MCID is a within-person change threshold and is not a tolerance for draw noise.
4. **Unit of the verdict.** The pooled verdict becomes a per-model, per-framing verdict.
5. **Corpus.** v3 in place of v2 and the run1/run2 files. The clinical band flip is 35.19%, not 35.40%.
6. **Abstract.** "A single prompt changes severity category on a third of its repeated draws" misstates the statistic. The 35% is the probability that two draws differ. Suggested replacement: "Two draws of the same prompt fall in different PHQ-8 severity categories 35% of the time, and one draw falls outside its prompt's most common category 25% of the time; averaging 9 to 35 draws per prompt, depending on the model, gives a score dependable at .90." The body's "a single draw reproduces its cell's modal severity category 74.9% of the time" should read "a single draw falls outside its cell's most common severity category 24.9% of the time, where the most common category is taken over the cell's 30 draws under one framing". The published 25.1% pooled both framings into 60-draw cells and was a simulation estimate; the exact per-framing value is 24.88% (clinical) and 22.69% (narrative).
7. **Decoding control.** The control is read on variance components, not flip rates. This separates what temperature 0 removes (draw variance) from what it cannot supply (persona variance).
